How this works

One shelf for everything

The hub is the front door to all the AMS apps: one card per app, with its hand-drawn icon and a one-line reminder of what it does. Tap a card and the app opens.

List or grid

The button next to Settings switches the shelf between a list (best on the iPhone) and a grid of tiles (best on the Mac). The hub picks a sensible default for your screen and remembers your choice on each device. Everything works the same in both — the sections just sit in their own panels either way.

The little version chips

The small number on each web-app card is live — the hub quietly asks each app which version it's running right now, every time you open the hub. So the shelf doubles as a status board: if you just published an update, the new number shows up here. If a chip is missing, the hub couldn't reach that app (offline, or the app doesn't announce a version).

iPhone rule: open apps from their own icons

Each AMS app installed on your home screen keeps its data in its own private box. A link tapped inside the hub app can't open that installed copy — it would open a separate, empty one instead. That's why, when the hub runs as a home-screen app, tapping a web-app card first shows a friendly reminder to use the app's own icon. On the Mac, in Safari, cards simply open in a new tab and everything shares the same data — no reminder needed.

On the Mac

Timers and the Finance Hub run only on the Mac (they live on the Mac itself, not on the internet), so their cards are marked MAC ONLY. Tapping them on the iPhone does nothing useful — that's expected.

Where everything lives

Every card carries a small DATA line saying where that app keeps its stuff — so there's never any mystery about where things live:

  • On this device only — the app stores everything in the browser storage of the device you use it on (each device has its own copy; that's what the app's backup buttons are for).
  • Synced via Dexie Cloud — AMS Packing is the special one: its catalogue lives on each device and syncs between iPhone and Mac through a Dexie Cloud account.
  • Excel files — the Finance Hub doesn't store anything itself; it watches the monthly Excel workbooks and ledgers in the AMS Finance folder in Documents.
  • The Obsidian vault — Sam, the robot assistant, keeps notes and memory as plain files in the Obsidian vault on the Mac.
  • The apps' code is public on GitHub — but none of your data ever is.

Your links

The bottom section is yours: add any web address you want on the shelf — dashboards, sites, anything. These links are stored only on this device, never in the hub's public code, so private addresses stay private. The pencil on a link edits or deletes it.

Backups

Under Your data below you can save your links as a small backup file, copy them to the clipboard, or restore a backup. A restore shows its work: it tells you exactly how many links came back, and if anything goes wrong your current links and your backup file are left untouched.

Updates

The hub checks for a new version of itself when you open it (and whenever you come back to it, at most every 5 minutes). When a newer version is live, a bar appears at the bottom with an Update button — one tap loads it. The Check for app update button below asks immediately.

Your data

Your personal links live only on this device. Every now and then, save a backup file you can keep safe or move to another device.
